# Customer_purchase_repo
urning customer data into actionable insights with Power BI.


#Dashboard Highlights
Customer Insights – Analyze customers by age group and demographics.
Purchase Analysis – Track total purchases across different product categories.
Subscription Analysis – Compare subscribed vs. non-subscribed customers.
Customer Ratings – Monitor average customer ratings.
Seasonal Trends – Understand purchasing and purchase frequency across seasons.
Category Performance – Identify the most and least purchased product categories.
Purchase Behavio– Analyze previous purchases and purchase frequency.

#Tech Stack
Python – Used for data processing, cleaning, and preparing the customer purchase dataset.
Pandas – Used to load, clean, transform, and analyze the dataset efficiently.
NumPy – Used for numerical calculations and data manipulation.
Power BI – Used to create interactive visualizations, charts, KPIs, and the final dashboard.
DAX – Used to create calculated measures and perform analytical calculations in Power BI.
